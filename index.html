Working Process

A fixed four-phase sequence. No shortcuts skipped.

Every UMS project runs through the same engineering discipline, regardless of scale.

PHASE 01

Audit, Diagnostics & System Design

Site evaluation, shading analysis, and historical utility bill tracking establish an exact baseline. PV layouts are drafted against structural weight capacity and optimal solar radiation angle — not generic templates.

PHASE 02

Hardware Sourcing & Compliance

Tier-1 high-efficiency panels, instrumentation-grade electronics, high-end inverters, and weather-resistant mounting hardware — sourced in full alignment with net-metering policy and international safety standards.

PHASE 03

Precision Deployment & Smart Integration

Physical and electrical installation executed by certified engineering crews, followed by integration of real-time power dashboards to measure generation (kWh) and log utility savings from day one.

PHASE 04

Lifecycle Maintenance & Optimization

Planned maintenance agreements, precise panel cleaning protocols, and electrical health audits protect system longevity long after commissioning.
